About Me

I am a Research Fellow at the Auckland Bioengineering Institute, The University of Auckland, where I lead two externally funded projects as PI. I am also Principal AI Engineer at Determ Technology, where I lead the development of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models for robotics. I received my Ph.D. in Intelligent Science and Technology from Tongji University in 2025. My research sits at the intersection of computer vision, embodied AI, causal inference, and biomedical engineering.

Research Interest

My current research focuses on 3D spatial understanding, causal inference, and embodied AI. I am especially interested in intelligent robotic systems that can perceive, reason, and act safely in complex physical and clinical environments.

Supported by an HRC Explorer Grant, I develop physics-constrained AI for marker-less tumour localisation in breast surgery. The approach embeds biomechanical models of soft-tissue deformation to track the tumour in the deformed breast with calibrated uncertainty. It builds on my work on real-time soft-tissue segmentation, reconstruction, and deformation-aware tracking for mixed-reality surgical guidance. I also design LLM-based multi-agent systems for smart glasses that combine reasoning with physiological sensing for context-aware health feedback.

In an industry collaboration with the world's second-largest short-video platform, I combine small world models with causal inference for large-scale recommender systems, estimating supply value and cannibalization effects.

At Determ Technology, I build a closed-loop engineering framework for VLA models in robotic manipulation. It covers data curation, automated data auditing and annotation, human-in-the-loop RL + VLA training, and real-robot RGB/RGB-D deployment. Part of this work is released as the open-source Loop-Engineering-for-VLA toolkit (700+ GitHub stars).
